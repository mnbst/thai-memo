Write a "hook" for the chosen sentence: the first line of the post.
The post is laid out as: hook -> Thai -> pronunciation -> English translation. The hook makes people who cannot read Thai stop scrolling.
- Shape: name the moment, then the role of the line. e.g. "When you want it less spicy", "What to say when your boss asks you to stay late", "How to turn down a second drink"
- English only, at most 45 characters, ideally around 30. One scene only; cut filler words. No Thai script, no hashtags, no "in Thai" phrases
- Make it clear who says it and in what situation (use the candidate's usage notes)
- Do not repeat the translation. The translation comes last, so do not give the answer away
- Do not add the 👇 emoji; the code adds it
- For BL dramas, never name the show or the characters. Describe only the scene and the relationship between the two. Refer to the other person by that relationship (senior, junior, boyfriend, childhood friend, etc.)
